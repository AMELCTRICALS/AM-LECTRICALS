Helpful information

Questions? Start here.

A few quick answers to help you make an enquiry with confidence.

How do I request a quote?

Fill in the quick enquiry form and select “Prepare WhatsApp Enquiry”. WhatsApp opens with your details ready to review. Check the message and press Send when you are happy with it.

Are prices shown on the website?

Prices are not listed because each job can differ. Share the work required and ask for an estimate, including labour and materials where relevant, before confirming.

Can I ask about a job not listed here?

Yes. Choose “Other home improvement” in the enquiry form and describe what you need. AM ELECTRICALS can then let you know whether it is a service they can take on.

Do I have to book immediately?

No. You can ask questions and discuss the work first. Confirm the job only after you understand the scope, estimate and availability.

What should I include in my message?

Include your area or locality, the problem, any relevant measurements or product details, and when you would prefer the work to be done. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information.
